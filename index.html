01 / ABOUT

关于这个空间A space for exploration

这里不试图给出一个固定的自我定义，而是展示持续学习、思考与创造的过程。Not a fixed definition of a person, but an evolving record of learning, reflection and creation.

“保持好奇，保持独立思考，保持行动。”“Stay curious. Think independently. Keep creating.”

我对世界始终抱有好奇。知识不应只停留在阅读和记忆里，也应该帮助我们理解现实、提出问题，并尝试解决问题。这个网站是一个开放的个人档案：记录值得保存的想法，整理认真完成的作品，也记录认知不断变化的轨迹。I remain curious about the world. Knowledge should go beyond reading and memorization: it should help us understand reality, ask better questions and attempt useful work. This site is an evolving archive of ideas, projects and changing perspectives.
